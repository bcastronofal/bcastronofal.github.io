Bastián Castro Nofal

I'm an economist working as an Instructor and Research Assistant at the Department of Management Control and Information Systems of the University of Chile.

My research interests are mainly in Public Economics and Public Finance, with a focus on optimal taxation, income and wealth inequality, and public policy. I am also interested in Labor Economics.

My CV is available here, and you can contact me at bcastron [at] fen [dot] uchile [dot] cl.
